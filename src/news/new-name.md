---
title: "The game has a new name"
date: 2026-10-26
summary: Troop Master is now called Lord of the S'mores. Same game, same troop, same camp.
---
**Troop Master is now called Lord of the S'mores.** Another company already holds the name Troop Master, so the game needed its own before it goes on Steam. Nothing else about it changes: a troop of Wilderness Scouts, a camp to build by day, and a long night to hold it.

**In the game,** the menu and the window carry the new name, and the scouts and the guided first day now call you their Troop Leader. The next playtest build starts fresh: saved runs, progress and settings from Troop Master don't carry over.

**On this site,** every page and the press kit use the new name. If you signed up for the email list, you don't need to do anything.
