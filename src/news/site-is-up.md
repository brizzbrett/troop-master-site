Troop Master now has a website. It's a survival game about a troop of Wilderness Scouts camped somewhere they should not be: a colony sim by day, a tower defense by night.

There's an email list on every page. Sign up and you'll get one email when there's something to play: a demo, a store page, a release date. Nothing else.

Next up is version 0.2.0, the biggest update yet. It goes to the playtesters first, and a post about it will go up here.
